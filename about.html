The Genesis

The Story Behind Mr.Artist Studio

Mr.Artist was born from a fundamental frustration with modern interior art. For years, homeowners, interior architects, and apartment dwellers in Sri Lanka faced an uninspiring choice: either pay exorbitant sums for imported foreign decor, or settle for flimsy posters that bowed, curled, and discolored within months under tropical humidity.

Our founder and lead artist, Ganusha Dulanga, set out to solve this material and aesthetic dilemma. Combining a background in digital design, architectural balance, and material science, Ganusha experimented with dense composite backing substrates, UV-resistant pigment coatings, and modular split-panel configurations. The result was our hallmark 4mm (2600GSM) rigid fine art board and signature 3-panel triptych landscape collections.

Today, Mr.Artist Studio serves design enthusiasts across Sri Lanka and internationally, transforming living rooms, creative agencies, executive boardrooms, and boutique hospitality spaces with artwork that commands attention without overwhelming its architectural environment.
