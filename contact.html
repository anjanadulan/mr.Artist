Frequently Asked Questions

How is the artwork delivered?

We deliver island-wide across Sri Lanka using express domestic couriers within 2–4 business days. All boards are packed in double-walled protective cartons with reinforced corners.

Do I need frames for the 4mm rigid boards?

No framing is required. The 4mm (2600GSM) rigid boards are structural, bend-proof, and designed to mount directly flush to the wall using the pre-applied heavy-duty adhesive pads.

What payment options are accepted?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) island-wide and Direct Bank Transfers. International patrons can pay securely via Wise or PayPal.

Can I request custom dimensions?

Yes! Visit our Custom Studio or message our WhatsApp desk with your exact wall dimensions.
