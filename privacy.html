Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026 • Applies to Mr.Artist Web Platform & Mobile Applications

1. Overview & Commitment

Mr.Artist Studio ("we", "our", or "us"), owned and operated by Ganusha Dulanga, is committed to safeguarding user privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy details how information is collected, utilized, and protected across our website (https://mr-artist.vercel.app) and our associated digital services.

2. Information We Collect

• Voluntary Customer Inquiries: When you initiate an artwork commission or custom spec poster order via WhatsApp or email, we receive voluntarily supplied information such as contact details, reference imagery, custom dimensions, and shipping addresses necessary to fulfill your order.
• Automated Log Data: Like most modern web platforms, our servers and analytics automatically capture standard technical diagnostics including browser type, operating system, IP address, referral URLs, and session timestamps.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Disclosures

We partner with third-party advertising vendors, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. To comply with Google Publisher Policies, please note the following:

  • Third-Party Vendor Cookies: Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites across the Internet.
  • Advertising Cookies: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve personalized advertisements to our users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opting Out of Personalized Advertising: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, users may opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • How Google Uses Data: For comprehensive information regarding how Google processes information when you visit partner sites, please review How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps.

4. Cookie Policy & Web Beacons

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by websites that you visit. We utilize cookies and similar tracking technologies (such as web beacons and local storage) to ensure site functionality, preserve user preferences (such as currency display in LKR or USD), analyze traffic patterns, and deliver relevant ad experiences through certified networks. You can configure your browser to block or alert you about cookies; however, some site features may experience reduced functionality.

5. Data Security & Storage

Our platform inventory, database, and asset distribution are hosted on enterprise-grade infrastructure managed by Supabase and Vercel. We enforce strict HTTPS encryption across all client-server communication to protect data in transit.

6. Rights Under Global Regulations (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you possess rights to request access to, rectify, or erase personal data held by us. You also maintain the right to object to or restrict processing of your information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the details below.
