Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026 • Applies to Mr.Artist Web & Mobile App

1. Overview

Mr.Artist Studio ("we", "our", or "us"), owned and operated by Ganusha Dulanga, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you visit our website (https://mr-artist.vercel.app) or use our Android mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

• Direct Communications: When you initiate a custom poster request or artwork order via WhatsApp or email, we receive your voluntarily provided contact details, vehicle/character references, and order notes to fulfill your request.
• Log & Device Data: Like most web and mobile applications, our servers automatically log non-personally identifiable information such as browser type, operating system version, device model, and referring URLs.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense & AdMob)

We use Google AdSense on our website and Google AdMob in our mobile application to serve advertisements. Google and its certified ad partners use cookies and device advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID) to serve personalized and non-personalized ads based on prior visits.

You can manage or opt-out of personalized advertising anytime by visiting Google Ads Settings or adjusting your device's privacy settings.

4. Data Storage & Security

Our catalog inventory and artwork assets are hosted securely via Supabase and Vercel infrastructure. We employ industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/TLS) to secure all data transmissions.
